Planet surface textures
=======================

Earth (muted derivative), Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus,
Neptune, and Moon textures are from Solar System Scope:
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

They are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
License (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The Earth texture was resized and colour-muted for Mindsphere. The other
textures were resized.

All other textures in this folder (Pluto, the exoplanets, geodesic pattern,
solid colours, gradients, and star maps) are procedurally generated for
Mindsphere and are released under the project's MIT license.
